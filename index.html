I’m Cameron Murray, a graphic designer (among other things) with a knack for jumping between branding, digital design, motion and whatever else a project throws my way. I like mixing creative thinking with a bit of tech to make work that’s both thoughtful and engaging.

New Junction

Midland’s seldom used oval laying at its heart was primed for transformation into a new meeting place that would become the town’s new backyard. The New Junction brand aims to bring new life and energy to the heritage of Midland.

Black Swan 2020 Season Campaign

Black Swan State Theatre's 2020 Season campaign, Stories from Here, was created to take audiences on a road trip around Western Australia. Taking inspiration from scrapbooks, travel journals, road directories and, even old telephone books, with colour sampled directly from the land.

WAnderland

WAnderland is the down under answer to Alice in Wonderland. A playground for people who have never been able to leave a locked room alone, a page unturned, or an over grown trail ignored.

The Fame Project

Talent and drive know no boundaries, but unfortunately opportunity can. FAME needed a brand to bring film, art, music and entertainment technology and expertise to remote communities all in a state-of-the-art music and video studio; on the back of a truck.

TAKE1 Youth Screen Festival

A creative festival for the smartphone generation of aspiring filmmakers. From the name to its identity – TAKE1 Youth Screen Festival ditched the tired film clichés and embraced new media to aim itself at its young, always-on audience.
