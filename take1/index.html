Challenge

Launching a creative festival to the most cynical generation marketing has ever known is no mean feat. But that was exactly what the City of Mandurah aimed to do. Before it could even launch, the festival required significant buy-in to go ahead from the broader council, a youth reference group, stakeholders and sponsors.

Solution

We collaborated with the City to build a compelling strategy, name and identity to get the project green-lit.

From the name to its identity – TAKE1 Youth Screen Festival ditched the tired film clichés and embraced new media to aim itself at its young, always-on audience.

A comprehensive website and awards platform was created in Webflow, allowing all submissions to be displayed on the TAKE1 website.
