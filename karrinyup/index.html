Challenge

We were briefed to drive preference for Karrinyup, increase average spend of customers and elevate the brand in line with Karrinyup’s vision, at a time when the people of Perth were coming out of the confines of COVID and emerging from what felt like the longest winter.

Solution

Aligned with Karrinyup’s own metamorphosis – they are currently in the process of a major renovation – so too were the people of Perth. Everyone was looking for a reason to celebrate. We sought to express this joy in Karrinyup Shopping Centre’s SS20/21 campaign idea – ‘Renew You’.

After the creation of the campaign idea, I oversaw the entire execution from directing the photoshoot and contributing to the styling, to the editing and compositing for the final imagery.

The final shoot and the installation involved over 1,000 hand folded butterflies that I designed - I even folded a few hundred of them.
