The FAME Project

Client

FAME

Involvement

Brand Identity

Challenge

Talent and drive know no boundaries, but unfortunately opportunity can. Remote Western Australia is home to cultures as old as history, yet the children who live in remote areas are limited in their opportunity to access the technology to express themselves and engage a wider audience. Enter The FAME Project. A Federal Government backed initiative to bring film, art, music and entertainment technology and expertise to remote communities all in a state-of-the-art music and video studio; on the back of a truck.

Solution

I was charged with bringing The FAME Project concept to life in a way that would pique the interest of corporate partners with a view to attracting on-going funding beyond the seed funding provided by the government. But it had to be a brand that would appeal in a genuine way to the kids who it was created to serve. The resulting brand operates on two levels. The ‘serious’ corporate brand being co-opted by the ‘creative’ entertainment brand that takes its cues from pop subcultures.
