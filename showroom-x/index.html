PADC Skull Awards 2021 Bronze — Brand Scheme Below $20,000

Challenge

We worked with Showroom-X to launch an exclusive online retail fashion brand to the Chinese market at a time when the retail landscape is changing. In Australia, the failure rate for retailers has reached 1.5X the national average compared to all sectors, while 1 in 10 purchases in Australia is now made online. Brands operating in Australia are experiencing the constraints of a market at saturation point.

Solution

I worked with Showroom-X to develop a sophisticated Brand Identity and creative platform for launch that connected Showroom-X with a Chinese audience. It was created using a contemporary Australian aesthetic in line with the high-end Australian product offering. The Brand Identity was developed to bring to life an international feel with our unique Australian accent. I also developed a website concept and created an investor presentation video.

The key deliverable after creation of the brand identity was the comprehensive planning and design of the website across mobile and desktop platforms.

Results

Showroom-X beat their first-round funding targets. At 2019 Paris Fashion Week, Showroom-X signed up their remaining targeted Australian designers – in some cases those who stock their product on no other online portal. They have now evolved into WeWearAustralian and have a store in SoHo New York City.
