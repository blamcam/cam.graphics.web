Welcome to the Road Train Domain

Client

Main Roads WA

Involvement

Storyboarding

Animation

Challenge

Driving outside Perth comes with a different set of challenges. Regional WA is full of long, open roads and road trains, which are essential to keeping the state moving. But for drivers unfamiliar with country roads, knowing how to safely share the road with these trucks can be tricky. Main Roads WA needed a campaign to educate city drivers on the basics of country driving and help keep everyone safe.

Solution

Together with Block, we created the Road Train Domain, a campaign built around three simple rules for safely sharing the road with road trains. The idea was to establish regional WA as a different driving environment, where understanding these rules is essential. We brought the campaign to life through out-of-home advertising and radio across regional WA, reaching drivers where they were most likely to need the information.

Under Block's direction, I storyboarded and animated the feature ad for the campaign and helped create the key assets for the rest of the rollout.
